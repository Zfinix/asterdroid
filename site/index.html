Support

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app and the agent are Apache-2.0. Relay, coming soon, will be an optional $5 a month. You only pay a model provider if you pick a paid one.

Do I need to root my phone?

No. Droid reads the screen and taps through Android's accessibility service. You install it as an APK from GitHub.

What does the model see?

The screen text and the screenshots the agent asks for. With the model on your own laptop, that stays on your network. With a cloud model, it goes to that provider.

Will it spend my money or message people?

Not without asking. It stops for a yes before it pays for anything or messages someone as you.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Android comes first.
