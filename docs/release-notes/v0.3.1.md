# Droid 0.3.1

*Your phone. Any agent.*

Experimental. `arm64-v8a` only. Fixes to talking to Droid over WhatsApp, iMessage and Telegram through Photon.

---

## Update

Download `asterdroid-0.3.1.apk` from the [assets below](#assets) and open it on the phone, or:

```sh
adb install -r asterdroid-0.3.1.apk
```

It installs over 0.3.0 and keeps your settings, keys, sessions and memory.

## Fixes

- **Approvals show the whole request.** Asking for a go-ahead over Photon no longer cuts the preview at 1,500 characters. A long one arrives over several messages with its indentation intact, and "Reply yes, always, or no." comes last, on its own. Droid only takes your answer once every part has arrived, so you never approve something you have not seen.
- **One turn per chat.** Two messages, or a message and a reminder, arriving together could start two turns in the same chat, each with its own agent. A chat now runs one turn at a time, and later messages join it or wait their turn.
